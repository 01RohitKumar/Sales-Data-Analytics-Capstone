# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Business Insights

### Insight 1: West Region Leads Sales and Profit
The West region generated the highest sales of $725,255.64 and the highest profit of $108,329.81. This indicates that the West region is a major contributor to overall business performance.

### Insight 2: Technology Is the Most Profitable Category
Technology generated $836,154.03 in sales and $145,454.95 in profit, making it the leading category for both metrics.

### Insight 3: Furniture Has a Profitability Challenge
Furniture generated $741,306.31 in sales but only $18,421.81 in profit. This suggests that its costs, pricing, product mix, or discount strategy should be reviewed.

### Insight 4: Some Transactions Are Loss-Making
A total of 1,869 records had negative profit. These transactions require further analysis to identify the products, regions, and discount levels associated with losses.

### Insight 5: Discounts May Be Associated with Lower Profit
Discount and profit have a negative correlation of -0.22. This indicates a weak negative relationship, but it does not prove that discounts directly cause lower profits.

### Insight 6: Sales and Profit Are Positively Related
Sales and profit have a correlation of 0.48, indicating a moderate positive relationship in this dataset.

## 2. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Improve Furniture Profitability
Review Furniture product costs, pricing, and discount levels. Evaluate profitability at the sub-category level before making changes.

### Recommendation 2: Review Discount Policies
Identify transactions with high discounts and low or negative profit. Test more targeted discount strategies and compare their impact on profit.

### Recommendation 3: Investigate Loss-Making Transactions
Analyze negative-profit records by region, category, sub-category, and discount. Use the findings to identify practical ways to reduce avoidable losses.

### Recommendation 4: Learn from the West Region
Investigate which products and business patterns contribute to the West region's strong results. Apply successful practices in other regions where appropriate.

### Recommendation 5: Monitor Performance with the Power BI Dashboard
Regularly track total sales, total profit, quantity sold, regional performance, category performance, and loss-making transactions. Use the dashboard filters to investigate specific areas.

## 3. Conclusion
The analysis shows that the West region and Technology category are major contributors to sales and profit. Furniture profitability and loss-making transactions deserve further investigation. The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive way to monitor key metrics and support data-driven decisions.

*Note: These findings describe patterns in the available dataset. Correlation does not establish causation, and recommendations should be validated with further analysis.*
